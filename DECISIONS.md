# The decisions of the release

IMPLEMENTATION.md §10 lists the questions that have to be answered before the
release. A recommendation is not a decision: each row below records what was
decided, why, and where the evidence is.

A decision that changes the contract of the specification is marked as such:
the specification itself is the normative document, so a change of the public
contract is recorded here and in the changelog of the release, never only in
the code.

| # | Question | Decision | Status | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Supported runtime versions | The gateway is built, tested and released on **Bun 1.4.3**; the contract states **Bun ≥ 1.4.3**. The 1.2 line was measured (1.2.0 and 1.2.23): it does not expose the early answer while the upload runs, it does not cancel the producer of the body, the `ws` client cannot observe the refusal of an upstream handshake and the upgrade of the caller with the headers of the gateway is refused. The measurement is in `docs/transport-capabilities.md` and the change of the contract is recorded in SPECS.md §15 (decision 21). | **resolved** | `docs/transport-capabilities.md`, `test/network/transport.test.ts` |
| 2 | Upload and response commitment boundaries | The observable events are: the **end of the request stream** (the upload is over), the **final response headers** (the answer of the upstream is committed) and the **first byte of the body**. The acceptance of the bytes by the peer is not observable and is not claimed. The commitment of the answer to the caller happens when the final headers are handed downstream; a failure after that ends the body instead of replacing the answer. | **resolved** | `test/network/transport.test.ts` (the phases, the upload, the early answer) |
| 3 | Upstream websocket non-101 / refusal mapping | The refusal of the upstream handshake is reported apart from a network failure: the adapter returns the status, the headers and the body of the refusal. The caller of the gateway receives `502 bad_gateway` with the status of the refusal in the log; the body of the upstream is not forwarded to the caller. A handshake that times out is `502 bad_gateway` as well, and the reservation of the slot is released. | **resolved** (the mapping is implemented in commit 11) | `test/network/transport.test.ts` (the refusal, the timeout) |
| 4 | Connection establishment during the upload | No extra ceiling is added. The upload is governed by the idle `bodyMs` budget only: a client that keeps making progress is never cut off by a total budget. The establishment of the connection to the upstream is part of the `responseMs` budget, which starts when the upload is over. | **resolved** | `test/network/transport.test.ts` (the upload), the exchange tests of commit 09 |
| 5 | Caller `Authorization` and the standard `Forwarded` header | The standard `forwarded` header is **removed** in both directions: it is the claim of a client about the request it made and it competes with the chain the gateway writes. The `Authorization` header of the caller is **forwarded** to the upstream unchanged: the upstream is an application of the same platform that the operator chose in the route table, the header is not one of the identity headers of the gateway, and the identity of the caller travels in the headers of the gateway and in the signature. The change of the removal list is recorded in SPECS.md §15 (decision 22). | **resolved** | `src/domains/forwarding/headers.test.ts` |
| 6 | Header representation | The roles are written as a comma separated list; a list without a role is an empty header value. A role that contains a comma or a control character is dropped from the header (it stays in the request to the access service, unfiltered). The identity values are truncated to 512 characters and the control characters are removed. The name of a cookie is case sensitive. | **resolved** | `src/domains/forwarding/identity-signature.test.ts`, `src/domains/forwarding/headers.test.ts` |
| 7 | Token purpose | The provider of the repository does not publish a token type claim, so the gateway validates the signature, the issuer, the audience, the expiry and the subject, and nothing else. The algorithms are the ones of the specification (`RS256`, `ES256`, `RS384`, `RS512`, `ES384`, `PS256`) and the tolerance of the clock is five seconds. A deployment whose provider publishes a token type claim adds the check in the authentication domain; the ports of the domain make it a local change. | **resolved** | `test/integration/oidc.test.ts` |
| 8 | Login transaction consumption | The state of the login is single use on four levels: the cookie of the state is deleted as soon as the callback is processed, the state of the callback has to be the state of the cookie, the verifier and the nonce bind the exchange to the login attempt, and the authorization code is single use at the provider. Two replicas that process the same callback at the same time both reach the provider, and the provider refuses the second exchange: the loser of the race gets `400 login_failed`. The state is therefore consumed, without a transaction of its own. | **resolved** | `src/domains/authentication/principal.test.ts`, `test/integration/oidc.test.ts` |
| 9 | Decision cache outage | Only an explicit decision of the access service is cached. A transport failure is never cached. When the cache is unavailable the gateway asks the access service again and denies when that fails. | **resolved** | `src/domains/workspace-access/workspace-access.test.ts`, `test/integration/access.test.ts` |
| 10 | Live websocket authorization | The websocket is authorized at the handshake only. The live connection is not revalidated when a session expires, when the access is revoked or when the configuration is reloaded. | open (commit 11) | websocket tests of commit 11 |
| 11 | Provisioning of the verifier of the signature | The upstream is given the *derived* key (the output of the HMAC of the secret), not the master secret. The key is versioned by the version of the signature (`v3`), so a future format can be told apart from it. | **resolved** | `src/domains/forwarding/identity-signature.test.ts` |
| 12 | External host and response caching | The gateway does not cache a response and does not add cache headers. An upstream that wants a response to be private has to say so itself. | open (commit 10) | contract tests of commit 10 |
| 13 | Replica topology and Redis TLS | The replicas share the sessions, the rate limits and the decision cache through Redis. The TLS of the endpoint is verified with the certificate authority of the deployment (`SHUTTLE_SESSION_TLS_CA_BASE64`). The caps of a process (sessions, connections, buffers) are per process. | open (commit 13) | deployment documentation of commit 13 |
| 14 | Drain policy | During the drain the readiness probe answers `503`, the listener stops accepting new work and the websocket connections are closed with the code 1001. The requests that are in flight get `SHUTTLE_SHUTDOWN_TIMEOUT_MS`. | open (commit 12) | shutdown tests of commit 12 |
